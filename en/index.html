06Coming in Dev 0.9.1

An AI that sleeps and remembers

Done in code, being tested before release. How it works and what our experiments showed is in the articles.

  • SleepAt night the model moves the day's conversations into a new layer without forgetting the old. In the morning it tests itself and shows what it learned.
  • ChatA separate screen for talking to the model. Chat during the day, and at night it falls asleep on schedule.
  • .vskOur own model file: nights as separate layers, the diary, the "I don't know" threshold, persona and answer settings. Any night can be forgotten, even after moving to another Mac.
